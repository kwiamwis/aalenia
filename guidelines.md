# Community Guidelines
* Avoid chronic or extreme toxicity
* Avoid chronic or extreme spam
* Do not use community resources to violate international laws or the laws of the United States
* Do not use the community Discord to violate the [Discord Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms), [Discord Community Guidelines](https://discord.com/guidelines), or the [Discord Discovery Guidelines](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/4409308485271-Discovery-Guidelines)
* Do not use the Path of Titans community servers to violate the [Alderon Terms of Service](https://alderongames.com/legal/terms-of-service) or [Alderon Server Hosting Agreement](https://alderongames.com/legal/server-hosting-agreement)
> Your speech privileges within the community may be rescinded upon violating the above stipulations. You will be expunged from the community upon chronic or extreme violation of the above stipulations.
