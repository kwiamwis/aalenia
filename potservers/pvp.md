# Aalenian PvP
The **Aalenian PvP** Path of Titans Server is PvP oriented, with easy yet balanced growth, marks, and teleportation.
## Mods
* Modded playables are added if they fit in with the PvP balance of vanilla playables. The assortment of modded playables changes often
* The Aalenian Utilities and Flora mods enhance the environment and gameplay
## Map and Environment
* This server is on the map Gondowa
* Modded trees and edible grasses and flowers can be found around the map
* The impact crater is typically flooded! The flood level changes occasionally, though droughts do occur
## Combat
* Characters spawn with spawn protection for a time, where they can neither take nor deal damage
* The rush of the hunt rewards a post-kill buff called 'Bloodlust'
## Growth
* The hatchling tutorial cave is disabled and you will first spawn as an adult
* Quest reward growth is multiplied by 0
* Characters always spawn as adults
* Set your character's growth stage if you are not in combat and you can edit abilities with `/Growth`, `/Grow`, or `/G`
* Toggle a temporary hatchling effect if you are not in combat and are able to edit abilities with `/Baby`
## Economy
* Quest marks rewards are multiplied by 5
* You may run `/Marks` to receive marks (per-character cooldowns apply), this command running automatically for a character every time it spawns
* Death removes 30% of your marks
* Store marks safely in per-character bank accounts accessible by visiting Banker NPCs found across the map
## Teleportation
* Waystones are transformed into solo-friendly Lodestones, allowing transport from one to another or to Telestones
* Telestones can be found across the map, allowing transport from one to another or to Lodestones
* Lodestone/Telestone teleportation requires that a character not be in combat
* Lodestone/Telestone teleportation enforces per-character cooldowns so the effects of molecular destabilization do not cause violent self destruction
* Lodestone/Telestone anti-camping measures may be enabled, depending on player behavior
* Successful teleportation applies temporary immunity where characters can neither take nor deal damage
* Custom spawn locations may be enabled, depending server occupancy
* Teleports from anywhere using `/TP` may be enabled, depending server occupancy
## Custom Skins
* Visit the Taxadermist NPC to precisely change the colors of your scales or feathers
## Stuck?
* `/U`, `/Unstuck`, and `/Unstick` will help you out if you are not in combat
## Objects of Interest
* Lunaristones which affect gravity and Regenistones which increase regeneration may be found around the map in various locations
* Crystals which instill various magical effects when eaten may be found across the map
## Rules
Simply do not violate the rules outlined in the [Community Guidelines](/guidelines)
