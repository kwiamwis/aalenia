# Aalenian Realism
The **Aalenian Realism** Path of Titans Server is realistic not because of rules but because of consequences. Permanent death and nearly no healing will force you to behave differently.
* The hatchling tutorial cave is disabled and you will spawn at 0 growth
* No teleportation of any kind, including waystones, is present
* Growth is passive when above 90% health, thirst, hunger, and oxygen
* Quests will not give growth
* Death is permanent
* Healing and bone break healing are 50x less powerful
## Rules
Simply do not violate the rules outlined in the [Community Guidelines](/guidelines)
