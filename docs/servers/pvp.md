# Aalenian PvP
The **Aalenian PvP** Path of Titans Server is PvP oriented, with growth and teleport commands enabled.
## Growth
* Set your growth stage with /growth, /grow, or /g
* Death will not remove growth
## Teleportation
* Custom spawn locations mean you never have to see rainbow hills again!
* Solo-friendly waystones are enabled, allowing transport from one to another. No more begging in global for a waystone!
* Custom teleport stones can be found accross the map, allowing you to go to waystones and other teleport stones.
* All teleportation requires a 30 second out-of-combat sleeping period
## Rules
Simply do not violate the rules outlined in the [Community Guidelines](/guidelines)
